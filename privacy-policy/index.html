1. Information We Collect

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and handled when you visit our website for the Confluenc Quant Indicator [v6.1.12]. We are committed to transparency and respect visitor privacy.

When you browse or interact with this website, the following types of information may be collected:

  • Technical & Log Information: Standard server logs automatically record details such as your internet protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, device hardware specifications, operating system, language preferences, referring URLs, pages viewed, and date/time stamps.
  • Website Interaction Data: Information regarding how you navigate our landing page, including page dwell time, scroll depth, and interaction events such as clicking the primary DOWNLOAD button.
  • Information Provided During Checkout: Our website does not maintain user account registrations or directly collect credit card numbers. When you click the DOWNLOAD button, you are redirected to our verified external checkout platform (SuperProfile), where your contact details and TradingView username are collected to process your order and provision indicator access.

2. Website Analytics & Meta Pixel Tracking

This website utilizes analytical tracking tools, including the Meta Pixel (provided by Meta Platforms, Inc.), to measure website traffic, evaluate campaign performance, and understand how visitors interact with our page.

How Meta Pixel Works: The Meta Pixel loads a small snippet of code in your browser that records standard events such as PageView upon visiting the site and interaction events (such as DownloadClick and InitiateCheckout) when the DOWNLOAD button is clicked. This data allows us to measure advertising effectiveness and optimize our marketing reach without profiling sensitive personal activities.

Meta may match collected event data with your Meta account profile in accordance with Meta's Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Meta manages user data and manage your ad preferences by visiting Meta's official ad settings.

3. Cookies & Similar Technologies

We and our analytical partners use cookies, web beacons, and local browser storage to provide essential technical functionality, remember visitor preferences, and collect anonymous aggregate traffic data.

  • Essential & Functional Cookies: Necessary for the secure and reliable operation of the website, cache management, and asset delivery.
  • Analytics & Measurement Cookies: Used by tools like the Meta Pixel to recognize returning visitors, evaluate traffic sources, and record conversion pathways.

You can configure your browser to reject cookies, alert you when a cookie is placed, or delete existing cookies through your browser settings. If you disable cookies, you can still view the website and access the external checkout without restriction.

4. How Information is Used, Stored & Protected

Information gathered through the website is used exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Maintaining website availability, monitoring server health, and ensuring optimal load speeds across mobile and desktop devices.
  • Measurement & Optimization: Analyzing visitor engagement trends and evaluating marketing campaign performance.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: Detecting, preventing, and responding to malicious traffic, fraudulent transactions, or security breaches.

Data Security: We employ industry-standard technical measures, including Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption for all web communications, to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Payment transactions are never processed on our local landing page servers.
