Privacy Policy

How we collect, protect, and handle your order information and TradingView credentials.

Confluenc Quant [v6.1.12] Effective Date: October 9, 2026 Last Updated: October 2026

Commitment to Privacy: Confluenc Quant operates with complete transparency. We collect only the minimum required information necessary to provision your invite-only TradingView indicator access and provide customer support. We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or purchase access to Confluenc Quant Indicator [v6.1.12], we collect the following types of information:

  • Customer Contact Details: Name, email address, and telephone number provided during the checkout process to communicate order confirmations and activation updates.
  • TradingView Username: Your public TradingView account username, collected solely to grant your private invite-only script authorization directly on TradingView.
  • Order & Transaction Metadata: Payment reference ID, order timestamp, license version, and billing amount. Note that all financial details (such as credit card numbers, debit card details, UPI IDs, and bank account credentials) are processed exclusively by our PCI-DSS compliant checkout provider Superprofile. We never store or view your payment instruments.
  • Technical Interaction Data: IP address, device type, operating system, browser version, and aggregated usage metrics collected via standard server logs and analytical cookies.

2. How We Use Customer Information

We use the information collected strictly for specified, lawful business purposes:

  • Script Access Provisioning: Authorizing your verified TradingView username on our private PineScript repository so the indicator appears in your TradingView "Invite-Only Scripts" tab.
  • Installation & Technical Support: Providing step-by-step setup assistance, answering configuration inquiries, and resolving technical activation issues.
  • Product Updates & Alerts: Notifying active license holders of critical algorithm updates, version releases, or platform compatibility announcements.
  • Fraud Prevention & License Integrity: Verifying order authenticity and preventing unauthorized reselling, sharing, or misuse of private script authorizations.
  • Campaign Performance Analysis: Evaluating aggregate website performance and advertising efficiency without profiling individual financial behaviors.

3. Data Protection & Security Architecture

We deploy robust organizational and technological safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS): All communications between your browser and our servers are encrypted using modern 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption.
  • Access Restrictions: Access to customer order records and TradingView authorization databases is strictly limited to authorized engineers who require access for license fulfillment.
  • Zero Financial Storage: No credit card numbers, CVVs, net banking passwords, or UPI PINs are ever captured, transmitted through, or stored on our website servers.

4. Information Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personally identifiable information to third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared exclusively with essential infrastructure providers:

  • Payment & Checkout Partner: Superprofile (Bio Digital Platform) processes payments, generates automated invoices, and verifies transaction success.
  • Chart Visualization Platform: TradingView, Inc. receives your public TradingView username strictly through the platform's native invite-only permissions system to grant indicator access.
  • Measurement & Analytics: Anonymous, aggregated website interaction data may be processed via Meta Pixel (Facebook Ads) for advertising performance measurement. Users may opt out of third-party cookie tracking via browser privacy settings.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, subpoena, court order, or governmental regulation.

5. Data Retention Policy

We retain customer order records and TradingView username authorizations for the duration of your product ownership to ensure uninterrupted lifetime script access, facilitate re-authorizations (such as when switching TradingView accounts), and provide ongoing support. General server access logs are purged periodically after 90 days.

6. Customer Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and applicable consumer data protection regulations, you possess the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we maintain regarding your account and orders.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to inaccurate or outdated contact information or request that your indicator authorization be transferred to an updated TradingView username.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request the deletion of your personal records from our customer database, provided that license verification and legal tax record obligations are satisfied. Note that complete deletion may terminate your ability to receive future script updates.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may opt out of non-essential email notifications at any time.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience, remember session preferences, and analyze landing page conversion rates. You can configure your browser to reject cookies or notify you when cookies are being set. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to view the indicator demonstration or complete checkout.
