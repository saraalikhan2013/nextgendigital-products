Refund Policy

Clear standards regarding digital script delivery, installation support guarantee, and transaction resolution.

Confluenc Quant [v6.1.12] Effective Date: October 9, 2026 Last Updated: October 2026

Digital Product Transparency: Confluenc Quant Indicator [v6.1.12] is delivered immediately via private invite-only script provisioning on TradingView. Our policy is structured to be transparent, fair, and compliant with digital goods consumer protection standards.

1. Digital Goods Delivery & Nature of Service

Confluenc Quant Indicator [v6.1.12] is an intangible, digital software product delivered via private invite-only script authorization directly on the TradingView platform. Because proprietary algorithmic formulas and instant script access privileges are irrevocably granted to your TradingView account upon purchase fulfillment, purchases are generally non-refundable once access has been successfully granted, subject to applicable statutory consumer protection rights.

2. Dedicated Installation & Technical Support Guarantee

We are fully committed to ensuring that every licensed customer experiences seamless operation of Confluenc Quant Indicator on their TradingView charts. Before considering any order cancellation, our support team provides comprehensive installation assistance:

  • Step-by-Step Activation Guidance: Detailed visual walkthroughs demonstrating how to add the script from your TradingView "Invite-Only Scripts" tab.
  • Account Troubleshooting: Resolving TradingView username mismatches, capitalization discrepancies, or account permission sync delays.
  • Platform Configuration: Guidance on configuring multi-timeframe overlays, activating EMA ribbons, and setting up TradingView alerts.

3. Eligible Refund Scenarios

While digital goods are generally non-refundable once activated, we honor full refunds under the following verified conditions:

  • Duplicate or Accidental Multiple Charges: If technical lag or checkout issues result in multiple billing transactions for the same order, all duplicate charges will be refunded immediately upon verification.
  • Non-Delivery of Access (Within 72 Hours): If you have provided a valid, active TradingView username and our fulfillment system fails to provision your script access within 72 hours of purchase confirmation, and our support team is unable to resolve the issue, you are entitled to a 100% full refund.
  • Irremediable Technical Defect: If the script experiences a persistent, verifiable technical defect within the underlying PineScript code that prevents basic charting functionality, and our technical engineering team cannot resolve the defect within 5 business days of your formal defect report.

4. Ineligible Refund Situations

To prevent abuse of proprietary digital software, refund requests will not be approved under the following circumstances:

  • Trading Losses or Market Outcomes: Confluenc Quant Indicator is an educational and analytical charting tool. It does not provide financial advice or guarantee trading profits. We do not issue refunds based on personal trading results or market volatility.
  • Change of Mind: Requests submitted simply due to a change of personal preference after receiving full access and proprietary documentation.
  • Failure to Provide Username: Situations where access could not be granted because the buyer refused or failed to provide their TradingView account username. (In such cases, access remains ready to be provisioned immediately upon username submission).
  • Third-Party Platform Discontinuation: Account restrictions or suspensions applied by TradingView, Inc. to a user's personal TradingView account unrelated to our script.

5. How to Submit a Support or Refund Claim

If you encounter billing discrepancies, duplicate transactions, or missing script access, please contact our dedicated resolution desk following these steps:

  1. Send an email to support@confluencquant.com with the subject line: [Support / Refund Request] — Order ID.
  2. Include your full name and the email address used during checkout.
  3. Attach your Superprofile payment receipt or transaction reference ID.
  4. Specify your exact TradingView username and describe the issue encountered in detail.

6. Processing Time & Method of Reimbursement

Approved refunds are processed through our payment provider Superprofile directly to the original payment method used during checkout (Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, or Net Banking). Refunds typically reflect in the customer's account within 5–7 business days depending on your banking institution's processing cycles.

Note on Script Revocation: Upon the approval and processing of a refund, all private invite-only script authorizations on TradingView associated with the refunded order will be automatically deactivated.
