Refund Policy

Confluenc Quant Indicator [v6.1.12] Effective: October 2026

1. Nature of the Digital Product

Confluenc Quant Indicator [v6.1.12] is an intangible, digital software product delivered via private invite-only script authorization directly on the TradingView platform, accompanied by online configuration resources and visual guidance.

Digital Delivery Terms: Because access to proprietary algorithmic code and script permissions is provisioned upon payment confirmation, digital access is generally non-refundable once access has been provided, subject to applicable mandatory consumer protection laws and statutory refund rights in your jurisdiction.

Before completing your purchase through the external checkout, please review the product description, requirements, and compatibility notes to ensure the indicator meets your technical analysis requirements.

2. Eligible Resolution & Refund Scenarios

We are dedicated to ensuring every customer receives the digital access they paid for. We review and honor resolutions and refunds in the following situations:

  • Duplicate Transactions: If a technical glitch, checkout timeout, or payment gateway delay results in duplicate charges for the same order, all verified accidental duplicate payments will be refunded promptly upon verification.
  • Missing Access or Failed Delivery: If you completed payment, provided an accurate and active TradingView username, and our team has not provisioned script access, or if the indicator cannot be accessed under your TradingView "Invite-Only Scripts" tab after contacting support, we will rectify the delivery or issue a full refund if delivery cannot be accomplished.
  • Unresolved Technical Failures: If you encounter a verified, persistent technical failure that prevents the indicator from functioning on your TradingView account and our technical assistance is unable to resolve the issue.

3. Ineligible Refund Scenarios

Refunds are not granted in the following circumstances:

  • Trading Results & Market Performance: Confluenc Quant Indicator [v6.1.12] is a technical chart analysis and educational software tool. It does not provide financial advice or guarantee trading profits. We do not issue refunds based on personal trading performance, market losses, or market volatility.
  • Change of Mind: Refund requests submitted simply due to personal preference, buyer's remorse, or changing interest after digital access has already been granted and activated.
  • Failure to Provide Account Details: Situations where script authorization is delayed because the customer has not provided an active TradingView account username. In such cases, access will be provisioned as soon as the username is provided.

4. Statutory Consumer Protection Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy is intended to limit, exclude, or modify any statutory consumer rights, mandatory cancellation periods, or non-waivable legal guarantees that you may be entitled to under applicable consumer protection legislation in your jurisdiction that cannot lawfully be excluded by contract.

5. How to Raise an Issue or Request Assistance

If you experience duplicate charges, missing script access, or technical activation difficulties, please reach out through the official support channel on the SuperProfile product page associated with your purchase:

Official Checkout & Support Channel: SuperProfile Confluenc Quant Page

When contacting support regarding a billing or delivery issue, please include:

  • Your order reference ID or transaction identifier from your checkout confirmation.

  • Your exact TradingView username (case-sensitive) to verify script authorization.
  • A clear description of the issue or error encountered.

Our support team will review your order details, check script authorization status on TradingView, and work with you to resolve the matter promptly.
