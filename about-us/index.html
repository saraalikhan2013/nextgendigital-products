About Confluenc Quant

A unified technical analysis tool engineered to organize complex chart studies into one clear, cohesive TradingView interface.

Confluenc Quant [v6.1.12] Platform: TradingView Engine: PineScript v5

Product Purpose: Confluenc Quant Indicator [v6.1.12] is an all-in-one technical analysis software tool designed for the TradingView platform. Instead of overcrowding charts with dozens of separate, conflicting scripts, Confluenc Quant integrates multi-period trends, institutional benchmarks, dynamic levels, and market structure into a single, high-clarity workspace.

1. Why Confluenc Quant Was Developed

Modern market analysis often suffers from analysis paralysis. Traders frequently apply multiple overlapping indicators—moving averages, pivot formulas, volume curves, and horizontal support bands—which consume valuable screen space, generate conflicting signals, and clutter the decision-making process.

Confluenc Quant Indicator [v6.1.12] was engineered to solve this fragmentation. It synthesizes essential mathematical market models into a synchronized framework where every tool complements the others, giving traders a structured, repeatable way to review market conditions.

2. Core Features & Analytical Systems

The indicator incorporates 10 dedicated technical analysis capabilities accessible directly from your TradingView chart:

Smart Trend System

A multi-period EMA ribbon (9, 21, and 50 period exponential moving averages) designed with dynamic color smoothing to visualize trend direction, momentum strength, and trend pullbacks instantly.

Automatic Trendlines

Algorithmic trendlines that automatically detect and connect structural pivot highs and lows, removing subjective manual drawing and highlighting structural trend breaks.

Smart Market Dashboard (HUD)

An on-chart real-time dashboard displaying key metrics including latest price, session percentage change, trend alignment status, and an aggregate confluence rating from 0 to 100.

Support & Resistance Zones

Dynamic horizontal key levels automatically plotted across the price scale to identify historical price reaction points, breakout barriers, and potential reversal areas.

VWAP Integration

Cumulative Volume-Weighted Average Price benchmark providing essential institutional fair-value reference for intraday equilibrium analysis and trend verification.

CPR Pivot Analysis

Complete Central Pivot Range analysis (Top Central, Central Pivot, Bottom Central, plus R1/S1 and R2/S2 levels) for range forecasting, trend conviction, and breakout identification.

Entry Zone Finder

Visual confluence highlighting that automatically identifies zones where multiple analytical factors converge to define structured potential trade setup parameters.

Smart Buy/Sell Signals

Algorithmic technical callout markers positioned directly above or below candlesticks to assist traders in identifying potential breakout and support bounce trade setups.

Up to 40 Smart Alerts

Pre-configured TradingView alert conditions for trend shifts, support/resistance tests, CPR crossings, and signal triggers sent directly to mobile apps, webhooks, or emails.

Multi-Timeframe & Market Support

Engineered for consistent operation across 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, and 1D timeframes in Cryptocurrencies, Equities, Indices (NIFTY / BANKNIFTY), Forex, and Commodities.
