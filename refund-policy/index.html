Installation Support Guarantee

If you experience any technical difficulties activating the indicator on your TradingView account, our installation support team is available to assist you step-by-step until your indicator is properly configured and operational.

Eligible Support & Refund Scenarios

While digital goods are generally non-refundable once activated, we honor full resolutions and refunds under the following verified conditions:

  • Duplicate Charges: If technical lag or checkout issues result in multiple billing transactions for the same order, all duplicate charges will be refunded immediately upon verification.
  • Missing Access / Non-Delivery: If you have provided a valid TradingView username and our team is unable to deliver script access within 72 hours of purchase confirmation, you are entitled to a full refund.
  • Unresolved Technical Issues: If you experience persistent delivery problems that our technical support desk cannot resolve after full troubleshooting.

Ineligible Refund Scenarios

  • Trading Losses: Confluenc Quant is an analytical charting tool and does not guarantee trading profits. We do not issue refunds based on personal trading results or market volatility.
  • Change of Mind: Requests submitted simply due to personal preference after receiving full access and setup resources.
  • Failure to Provide Username: Situations where access could not be provisioned because the buyer failed to provide their TradingView account username.
